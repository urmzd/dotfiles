# Alert or task name

- **Trigger:** the alert, page or request that starts this
- **Impact:** who is affected while it is unresolved
- **Owner:** team or role

## Diagnose

1. Command or dashboard, and what a bad result looks like.

## Mitigate

1. The fastest safe action, with the command.

## Resolve

1. The lasting fix.

## Escalate

When and to whom.
